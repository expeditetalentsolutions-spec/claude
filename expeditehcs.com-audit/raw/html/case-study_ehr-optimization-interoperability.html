HEALTH IT

Case Study: EHR Optimization & Interoperability Across a Multi-Hospital System

Summary: A multi-hospital health system operating across 5 states had grown through acquisitions, inheriting 6 different Electronic Health Record (EHR) platforms that could not communicate with each other. Clinicians were unable to access unified patient records, leading to duplicate tests, medication errors, delayed care transitions, and significant compliance risk.

Expedite Healthcare Solutions was engaged to optimize and unify the EHR landscape, implement interoperability standards, and deliver a seamless health information exchange across all facilities.

OVERVIEW

Client Goals

Unify Patient Records

Create a single longitudinal patient record accessible across all hospitals, clinics, and ambulatory sites regardless of the underlying EHR platform.

Eliminate Duplicate Data

Resolve duplicate patient records, conflicting medication lists, and fragmented clinical histories that were causing care gaps.

Enable Real-Time Data Exchange

Implement HL7 FHIR-based interoperability so lab results, imaging, referrals, and discharge summaries flow seamlessly between facilities.

Improve Clinician Efficiency

Reduce time spent searching for patient information, toggling between systems, and re-entering data across platforms.

Meet Regulatory Requirements

Achieve compliance with CMS Interoperability and Patient Access Final Rule, 21st Century Cures Act, and Meaningful Use requirements.

Reduce IT Overhead

Consolidate EHR support teams, eliminate redundant licenses, and standardize clinical workflows across the system.

Technology Alignment

HL7 FHIR R4 APIS

Standards-based data exchange enabling real-time communication between disparate EHR systems.

MASTER PATIENT INDEX (MPI)

Enterprise MPI with probabilistic matching algorithms to resolve duplicate records system-wide.

INTEGRATION ENGINE

Middleware platform (MuleSoft/Rhapsody) for routing, transforming, and monitoring clinical data flows.

CLINICAL DATA REPOSITORY

Centralized CDR aggregating records from all 6 EHR platforms into a unified patient view.

IDENTITY MANAGEMENT

Single sign-on (SSO) and role-based access controls for secure, streamlined clinician authentication.

CHALLENGE

The health system faced critical interoperability barriers:

6 Siloed EHR Platforms

Epic, Cerner, MEDITECH, Allscripts, athenahealth, and a legacy system — none natively interoperable, each with unique workflows and data models.

340,000+ Duplicate Records

Patient records existed in multiple systems with conflicting demographics, medications, and allergy lists — creating serious patient safety risks.

Care Transition Failures

When patients transferred between facilities, clinical information did not follow them, forcing clinicians to make decisions with incomplete data.

Regulatory Non-Compliance

The system was at risk of CMS penalties for failing to meet interoperability mandates and information-blocking provisions.

Clinician Frustration

Physicians and nurses spent an average of 45 minutes per shift navigating between systems, reducing direct patient care time.

SOLUTION

Strategic Approach

SYSTEM ASSESSMENT & MAPPING

Audited all 6 EHR platforms, documenting data models, clinical workflows, integration points, and regulatory gaps across every facility.

ENTERPRISE MPI DEPLOYMENT

Deployed a master patient index with AI-powered matching to identify, merge, and prevent duplicate records across the entire system.

FHIR-BASED INTEGRATION

Built HL7 FHIR R4 APIs connecting all 6 EHR platforms through a central integration engine, enabling real-time data exchange for labs, medications, allergies, and clinical notes.

UNIFIED CLINICAL PORTAL

Developed a single-pane clinical dashboard giving providers a consolidated patient view regardless of which EHR held the source record.

WORKFLOW STANDARDIZATION

Harmonized order entry, medication reconciliation, and discharge workflows across all facilities to reduce variation and error.

TRAINING & ADOPTION

Delivered role-based training for 2,800+ clinicians and support staff, with super-user programs and 24/7 go-live support for 90 days post-launch.

CONCLUSION

Results Achieved

92% Duplicate Record Resolution

Enterprise MPI resolved 313,000+ duplicate records, establishing a single accurate identity for every patient in the system.

35% Clinician Efficiency Gain

Unified portal eliminated multi-system navigation, saving an average of 35 minutes per clinician per shift.

Real-Time Data Exchange

Lab results, imaging reports, and discharge summaries now flow between all facilities in under 30 seconds.

Full Regulatory Compliance

Achieved 100% compliance with CMS interoperability mandates, 21st Century Cures Act, and Meaningful Use.

$6.8M Annual Savings

Eliminated redundant EHR licenses, reduced IT support overhead, and cut duplicate testing costs by 40%.

Zero Care Transition Gaps

Complete clinical data now follows every patient transfer, reducing adverse events during transitions by 67%.
