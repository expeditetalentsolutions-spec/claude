About Expedite Healthcare Solutions

Our Commitment to Excellence

Expedite Healthcare Solutions is a Joint Commission certified, minority- and woman-owned healthcare staffing and Health IT consulting company. We connect hospitals, clinics, and health systems nationwide with qualified travel nurses, allied health professionals, and clinical and non-clinical staff placing talent in an average of 5 days with a 97% retention rate. Our experienced recruiters and pre-credentialed talent pipeline help healthcare organizations maintain optimal staffing levels, improve patient outcomes, and modernize their technology infrastructure.
