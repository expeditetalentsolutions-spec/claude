QUALITY STANDARDS

Our Compliance Standards

In healthcare staffing, quality and compliance directly impact patient outcomes. Expedite Healthcare Solutions holds Joint Commission Certification, one of the healthcare industry's most recognized standards for quality and accountability. This certification reflects that our staffing, credentialing, screening, and placement processes are aligned with internationally recognized guidelines designed to support patient safety, workforce reliability, and compliant care delivery. By operating within these established benchmarks, we help healthcare facilities partner with a staffing provider whose processes have been independently evaluated and verified.
