ABOUT US

Get To Know Expedite Healthcare Solutions

Expedite Healthcare Solutions (DBA Expedite Talent Solutions) is a minority- and woman-owned business headquartered in Atlanta, GA, with a global presence across the United States, Canada.

With an extensive background in professional services, project solutions, and staffing, we offer the expertise of a global company combined with the one-to-one customized service of a local business partner.

Our consultative approach and wide range of specialized services empower clients to overcome obstacles and achieve rapid speed-to-market. By bridging the gap between technology and business processes, we accelerate our clients’ business objectives to create enduring value.

STAFFING & TECHNOLOGY SOLUTIONS

What We Do

Expedite is a global provider of staffing and technology solutions helping organizations from strategy to execution. Our disciplined yet flexible approach prioritizes clients’ goals while ensuring superior results. Our team of experienced consultants specializes in a variety of industry verticals, delivering high-impact solutions tailored to unique business challenges.
