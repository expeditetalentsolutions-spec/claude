9 · Decisions needed

Two things only you can unblock

Data

Search Console access

Or a 3-month export of Queries and Pages. It replaces the keyword guesses with real demand and ranks the work by traffic at risk.

Decision

expeditets.com vs. expeditets.co.in

If both sites have the same service pages, they compete in Google. Either give each site its own audience (US clients vs. India delivery and careers) or add hreflang en-US / en-IN.
